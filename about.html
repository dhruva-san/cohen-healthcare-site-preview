David Cohen — Healthcare Transactional Attorney.

Over two decades representing healthcare practice owners in the transactions that define their careers — purchases, sales, partnerships, and private equity roll-ups. That depth of experience now serves med spa, optometry, and veterinary owners navigating today's consolidation wave.

Two decades of healthcare transactions

Two Decades of Transactions. One Recognizable Pattern.

David Cohen has spent more than 20 years building Cohen Law Firm PLLC into a leading healthcare transactional law practice — closing hundreds of practice acquisitions, sales, partnership structures, and private equity roll-ups for practice owners across the country.

Across those two decades, David has been on the seller's side of the table for hundreds of transactions — negotiating the same large platforms, the same earnout and equity rollover structures, and the same post-close employment terms that define practice sales today.

That same consolidation wave is now breaking across veterinary, optometry, and medical spa practices. The buyers have names — NVA, VCA, Thrive, and Pathway in veterinary; Total Vision, Acuity, Keplr, MyEyeDoctor, and EyeCare Partners in optometry; PE-backed MSO platforms and aesthetics funds in med spa — but the underlying deal structures are the same across every fragmented, professionally-licensed healthcare sector.

Cohen Law Firm PLLC is built for exactly this work — a dedicated hub for these three verticals, backed by the transactional depth that made the firm a category leader.

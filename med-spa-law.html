Private equity transactions

Received an LOI from a PE Firm or MSO Platform?

Aesthetics-focused private equity funds, PE-backed MSO platforms, and regional strategic consolidators are actively acquiring medical spas — and regulatory complexity creates a real premium for sellers who have a specialist on their side of the table.

Before you sign anything — an NDA, an LOI, or a term sheet — get an independent review of the proposed structure. Med spa transactions carry an extra layer most other healthcare deals don't: the PC-MSO relationship itself often needs to be restructured or re-papered as part of the sale, which affects both valuation and timeline.

We review purchase price mechanics, earnout structures, equity rollover terms, and — critically — what happens to your role, your team, and your economics after the transaction closes.

Schedule Before You Sign

  1. 01
    Deal structure.

    Buyers often require the practice to be re-papered as part of closing — this affects valuation, timeline, and your economics. We make sure the structure works in your favor.

    Medical director continuity.

    If you're the medical director, your role and compensation post-sale need separate negotiation from the practice sale itself.

    Earnout & equity rollover.

    Reps & warranties.

    Buyers will ask you to make representations about the practice. We help you understand exactly what you're agreeing to — and negotiate the terms — before you sign.

Common questions

Med Spa Owner FAQs.

I've been approached by a PE-backed med spa platform. What's the first step?

Don't sign an NDA or LOI before an independent review. These deals move fast and the buyer's counsel has done this many times. We review the full proposed structure — purchase price, earnout, equity rollover, and your post-close role — and tell you exactly what is, and is not, negotiable before you respond.

How do I know what my practice is actually worth?

Valuation in a med spa sale turns on more than revenue — deal structure, earnout terms, and what happens to your team and your economics after closing all move the real number. We've been through hundreds of these transactions and help you see the full picture before you negotiate.

Do you represent buyers as well as sellers?

Yes. We represent practice owners on both sides of the table — acquiring a location or an entire group, or selling to a strategic buyer or PE platform. Having closed deals from both perspectives is exactly what lets us anticipate the other side's playbook.

How long does a med spa transaction take?

It depends on the size and complexity of the deal, but most practice sales and acquisitions run from a few weeks to a few months from LOI to close. After an initial review we'll give you a realistic timeline and flag anything likely to slow things down.

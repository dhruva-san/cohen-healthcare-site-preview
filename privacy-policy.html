Privacy Policy

Cohen Law Firm PLLC ('we', 'us', 'our') respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through healthcarepracticelaw.com, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

When you submit a contact form, schedule a consultation, or subscribe to our CPA deal memo, we collect the information you provide — including your name, email address, phone number, and any details about your inquiry.

How We Use Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry, schedule consultations, and — where you've opted in — send periodic updates such as our quarterly PE deal memo. We do not sell your information to third parties.

Cookies & Analytics

This site may use cookies and analytics tools to understand how visitors use our site and to improve content and navigation.

Your Choices

You may unsubscribe from email communications at any time. To request that we delete information you've submitted, contact us using the details on our Contact page.

Received an LOI from a PE-Backed Optometry Platform?

Total Vision, Acuity Eyecare, Keplr Vision, MyEyeDoctor, and EyeCare Partners are all active in the market — and the offers independent ODs are receiving today follow patterns that, to anyone who has handled hundreds of these deals, are highly recognizable.

Before you respond to an LOI, sign an NDA, or have a "casual conversation" with a platform's development team, get an independent read on what's actually being proposed. We represent the selling doctor — never the platform, never the buyer's counsel.

Our review covers the full deal structure: purchase price mechanics, earnout terms, equity rollover (if offered), and — often the most consequential piece — your employment agreement and operational role after closing.

Common questions

Optometry Practice Owner FAQs.

I received an LOI from a PE-backed platform. What's the first step?

Don't sign, and don't counter yet. The first step is a confidential review with independent counsel to understand the proposed structure — purchase price mechanics, earnout, equity rollover, and your post-close role. Most LOIs are non-binding on economic terms but set the tone for everything that follows.

How is selling to Total Vision, Acuity, Keplr, or MyEyeDoctor different from selling to another OD?

PE platform transactions include components a traditional doctor-to-doctor sale doesn't: earnouts tied to future performance, potential equity rollovers into the larger platform, and a multi-year employment agreement governing your role, compensation, and restrictive covenants after closing. Each needs to be negotiated as carefully as the purchase price itself.

What happens to my associate doctors if I sell to a PE platform?

This depends heavily on the deal structure and your associates' existing agreements. We review associate agreements as part of the sale process to understand what obligations transfer, what changes for your associates post-close, and how that affects the platform's valuation and your negotiating position.

Is it too early to talk to an attorney if I'm not actively selling?

It's actually the ideal time. Entity structure, associate and partnership agreements, and lease terms are far easier to put in order years before a sale than during a 30-45 day diligence window. An early conversation costs nothing and can materially improve your position when an offer does come.

How long does an optometry PE transaction typically take?

Most single-location transactions move from signed LOI to closing in 60-90 days. Multi-location groups take longer due to coordinated diligence across locations. We work to keep your timeline efficient without sacrificing the negotiation points that matter most to you.
